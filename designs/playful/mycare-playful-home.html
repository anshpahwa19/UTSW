UTSW MyCare home – playful direction

High-fidelity prototype of the patient app home screen. Bold rounded modules, spring feedback, and swipeable stacks for discovery – with the emergency alert and appointment details kept calm and clear. All people, dates, and alerts are fictional sample data.
