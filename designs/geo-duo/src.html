MyCare Duo

The location-aware home for a book-style foldable. Closed, it is a one-hand glance at where you are and what to do next. Open, your day and the map sit side by side. Fold or unfold at any step – your place is kept.

Concept device. “iPhone Duo” is not a shipping Apple product; sizes are assumptions. Location is simulated and all data is sample.

    Two postures, one day

    Closed, the cover is a glance: where you are, the stop you're at, and one primary action. Open, the left pane holds your day – pass, line, and Quick links – and the right pane gives the current stop a large map, its details, and the primary action at the bottom. Search, the tabs, and the menu are in reach either way.

    Pane rules around the hinge

    • Two panes of 390, each with 16.0 margins, so nothing sits within 16.0 of the crease (a 32.0 keep-out).
    • Only colour crosses the crease: the Blue band, backgrounds, and the scrim.
    • Tabs, sheets, search, and the system prompt live in the left pane; the primary action is pinned to the bottom of the right pane, level with the tab bar.
    • Directions open as steps on the left and the step map on the right.

    Folding keeps your place

    1. 0 msThe leaf lifts off the hinge. Under it, the map grows from where it sat on the cover.
    2. 450 msThe leaf is on edge. The cover turns away; the day line arrives on the left half.
    3. 900 msFlat. The map fills the right pane; from 630 ms, details and the primary action fade in.
    4. FoldThe same timeline backwards: the map shrinks into the cover's now card as the leaf closes.
    5. ReducedA 320 ms cross-fade. No turn, no growth.

    Journey states

      Trust and control

      • Never asks on launch. “Help when you arrive” explains first, then the simulated system prompt.
      • The location chip says what the home is using. Tap it for Not here?, Show the common homepage (pause), and Location settings.
      • Without location, everything works: choose a building, a spot inside, or a ZIP or city.
      • The cover's lock screen shows live directions – place, floor, suite, and step – never the department or the visit.
      • Check in, visit details, messages, results, visits, and billing open in MyChart.
