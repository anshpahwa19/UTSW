    Flow map

    From the home search to a shared provider profile

    One search, five scopes. Every entry point opens the same overlay; the Providers scope adds location, filters, and sort. Buttons jump the prototype to that step.

    Enter

    • Home search in the Blue header (All scope) and its top searches
    • Find my Care tile and Find my Care tab (Providers scope)
    • Near-me arrow – providers near you, by distance
    • Specialty list and search history, as in the old app

    Search

    • Suggestions as you type: specialties, conditions, names, places
    • Plain language and synonyms: “skin doc” → Dermatology; typos fixed
    • “near Frisco” sets the location; “Spanish-speaking” adds a filter
    • Recent (on this device) and popular searches when empty

    Refine

    • Location: near me, ZIP or city; fallback when denied or no fix
    • Filters: specialty, condition, distance, language, gender, hospital, new patients, online scheduling
    • Sort: best match, distance, last name A–Z, featured first

    Results

    • Cards: name, credentials, specialty, nearest location and distance, tags
    • Loading skeleton, error with retry, offline, and no results with ways out
    • Heart saves to this device; Undo in the toast

    Profile

    • Identity and credentials, bio, specialties and conditions, languages
    • Locations with directions and phone; education; research and video
    • Request appointment, call, save, share; related providers

    Act

    • Request: schedule online in MyChart, or call to schedule
    • Share: native share sheet with the public profile link only
    • Saved: Find my Care tab and the menu

    Guest tabs: Home · Find my Care · Locations · Wayfinding · MyChart

    The old app's five tabs, kept in spirit so returning users find everything where they expect it. Visits and Messages – the signed-in tabs in the playful direction – mean nothing to a guest, so they wait until sign-in.

    1. Find my Care replaces “Providers”: it is where people go with a problem, not a name, and it can grow to services and urgent care without a rename.
    2. Locations and Wayfinding stay separate: one answers “which place,” the other “how do I get there and around inside.”
    3. MyChart is sign-in only, plus Pay as a guest. Everything else from the old hamburger menu lives in the menu on the home app bar, as it did before, so the tab bar stays task-focused.

    Rules the flow keeps

    1. Search stays the hero: the same field, top searches, and overlay as the playful home; provider search is its Providers scope.
    2. Signed-in destinations (messages, results, visits, billing) never dead-end: they open a sign-in prompt that says what you'll get, with “Find a provider” for people who aren't patients yet.
    3. Guest favorites and recent searches live on this device only; nothing about a search is sent with a share.
    4. Location is asked for only when someone taps near me, never on launch.
