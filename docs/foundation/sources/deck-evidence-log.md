**Citation format used across the foundation documents:** `S1-<slide>` (for example, `S1-6` = slide 6).

## How to read this log

- **Deck section** is the blue header on the slide, quoted verbatim.
- **UTSW annotation** is the text the deck authors wrote on the slide. Bullets under "Current" headings are treated as **Confirmed** statements about the current app. Bullets under "Future" and "What We Want to Explore" are **Proposed** – they are not approved requirements.
- **Observed** is what is visible in screenshots. A screenshot of another organization's app is an *inspiration reference*, not a UTSW requirement. Organizations are named only where their name is visible on the slide.
- Interpretations are marked **Assumption**.

## Cross-slide observations

1. **Two navigation patterns in UTSW screenshots.** Five tabs (Home, MyChart, Providers, Locations, Wayfinding) on S1-1, S1-6, S1-8, S1-9, S1-13, S1-17 and S1-20. Three tabs (Home, Providers, Locations) on S1-14 and S1-15. Concepts on S1-17 and S1-18 use four unlabeled icons (home, search, map, profile).
2. **Two location models in UTSW screenshots.** "Find a Location" with Buildings / Clinics / Hospitals (S1-13), and "Clinics" with a Clinics/Maps toggle (S1-14).
3. **Existing cross-links between entities.** Provider → practice location (S1-6, S1-8); Building → Clinics in Building (S1-15); Clinic → providers ("Search Providers", S1-15).
4. **Formatting is inconsistent in current screens.** Phones use "(214) 645-4673" while S6 specifies 214-648-8400. Addresses appear as "Dallas, Texas 75390" and also "Fort Worth, Texas, 76104". Specialty names use a hyphen ("Dermatology - General") where S6 calls for a spaced en dash.
5. **No slide states business objectives, success metrics, user research findings, release scope, timelines or technical architecture.**
