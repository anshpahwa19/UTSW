**Notes:**
- **Only UT Southwestern Blue has an official screen value.** Any hex value for the other palette colors is an approximation and must be validated with the brand team before use.
- **Contrast (computed from the confirmed RGB):** UT Southwestern Blue `#004278` on white is about 10.2:1.
- **How "80% black" renders on screen is not defined.** It could be `#333333`, a CMYK-converted gray, or something else. The brand team needs to decide.
- **Several of the brights are likely to fail text-contrast thresholds on white.** This is an assumption to verify once digital values are confirmed.

## 2. Logo

- **The master logo has four approved versions (S5):**
  - Full color, the preferred version: "UT Southwestern" in PMS 2945 and "Medical Center" in 80% black.
  - All PMS 2945 blue.
  - All black.
  - Reversed white, only on a background of 75% black or darker.
- **Service line, Medical Group and Pediatric Group logos** follow the same rules (S5).
- **Clear space:** at least the height of the capital "U" on all sides (S5).
- **Minimum size:**
  - Print: 1.25 in. wide.
  - On screen: 187×30 px.
  - Retina displays: 108 px wide (vertical version) and 216 px wide (horizontal version) (S5).
- **The logo is custom artwork.** It is based on Helvetica Neue Heavy and Helvetica Neue Roman, but it must never be recreated with fonts. Always use the original digital art (S4, S5).
- **"UT Southwestern" must never be split across two lines,** in the logo or in text (S6).
- **Relevance to MyCare:** the "UTSW MyCare" wordmark in the current app (S1-17) and the circular "UTSW MyCare" badge in the concept (S1-17, S1-18) are not covered by S5. Whether there is an approved app lockup or icon is **Unknown** and needs to be confirmed with the brand team.

## 3. Typography

- **Only the logo typography is defined** (Helvetica Neue Heavy and Roman, S4).
- **No interface or body typeface is specified** in the materials provided (**Unknown**).
- **S6 says to avoid serif typefaces** for large blocks of web copy.

## 4. Editorial rules most relevant to the app (S6)

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Institution name | "UT Southwestern Medical Center" or "UT Southwestern" on first reference. "UTSW", "the Medical Center" or "the University" on second reference. |
| Hospitals | "William P. Clements Jr. University Hospital" on first reference, then "Clements University Hospital". Zale Lipshy Pavilion is written "Zale Lipshy Pavilion – William P. Clements Jr. University Hospital" when the location must be distinguished. |
| Affiliated institutions | UTSW faculty physicians care for patients at Clements University Hospital, UTSW clinics and affiliated institutions, including Parkland Health (Parkland Memorial Hospital for the building) and Children's Medical Center Dallas (Children's Health for the system). Use "Scottish Rite for Children". |
| Centers | Harold C. Simmons Comprehensive Cancer Center, then Simmons Cancer Center. Peter O'Donnell Jr. Brain Institute, then O'Donnell Brain Institute. |
| Degrees and titles | "Jane Doe, M.D., Professor of …" on first reference, then "Dr. Doe". Use periods in academic degrees (M.D., Ph.D.) but not in nonacademic ones (RN, FACS). |
| Dashes | No em dashes. Use a spaced en dash ( – ). |
| Serial comma | Always. |
| Numbers | Spell out one through nine. Use numerals for 10 and up, and always for percentages, ages and measurements. |
| Phone numbers | 214-648-8400; 1-800-222-2222. |
| Time | 8 a.m., 8:30–9 a.m., noon, midnight. |
| Dates | Abbreviate Jan., Feb., Aug., Sept., Oct., Nov. and Dec. with specific dates; spell out other months. |
| Headings | Title case for web headings. Headlines capitalize only the first word and proper nouns, with no period. |
| Ampersand | Only in official names, or to shorten web navigation. |
| Web writing | Paragraphs of three sentences or fewer. No "click here" or "learn more" link text. No directional characters on links (calls to action such as "Apply Now" are allowed). Bold and italics used sparingly. URLs written without "https://www". |
| Preferred spellings | Use healthcare, email, website, webpage, online, adviser, preventive, lifesaving, multidisciplinary, noninvasive, nonsurgical and well-being. Write "Ob/Gyn" (not "Ob/GYN"). Write "Neuro-Oncology". |
| Genes | Italicize gene names. Do not italicize protein names. |

### Known inconsistencies inside S6 (flag, do not resolve)

- **Time ranges:** the Time rule says ranges use a hyphen, but its own example ("8:30–9 a.m.") uses an en dash.
- **RNA:** the Acronyms exception allows "RNA" on first reference, but the Word Usage entry says to spell out "ribonucleic acid" first.
- **HTML tags:** the "Text Formatting" guidance names an `<emphasis>` tag, which is not a standard HTML element (`<em>` is).
- **Reversed logo:** S5 permits it only on 75% black or darker. Whether white on UT Southwestern Blue qualifies is not stated.
