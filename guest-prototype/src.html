    Spec

    One system, glass on brand blue

    The same tokens as the liquid glass home. This round adds the guest home rework and the Find care flow: search, location, filters, sorting, results states, the provider profile, saving, and sharing.

    Find care behavior

    • Plain language. “Itchy rash,” “heart doctor,” and “knee pain” map to a specialty. Abbreviations (PCP, GI, OB-GYN, PT) and misspellings (“dermatolgy”) map too, and the results say what was matched.
    • Filters. Choices in one group widen results (any). Groups narrow each other (all). Words like “Spanish-speaking” or “female” add a filter you can remove.
    • Sorting. Best match weighs fit, distance, and UTSW-approved featured providers, which always carry a Featured label. Nearest first needs a location.
    • Location. Near me asks first. If permission is denied or no fix is found, the ZIP or city field takes over with the reason in plain words.
    • States. Loading skeletons, no-match alternatives, a failing directory, and offline each have a message and a way forward.
    • Saved providers. Stored on this device for guests and readable offline. Sharing sends only the public profile link.

    Guest home, module by module

    • Kept from the old app: MedBlog stories with pause, quick links, UTSW resources (wayfinding, video visit prep), Find a provider by specialty, search history, and the city filter.
    • Quick links keep the old app’s four MyChart links with their exact names (Message your doctor, Get your test results, Manage your visits, Manage billing) and add Find my care and Locations. MyChart links are marked with a lock and open a sign-in explanation instead of dead-ending.
    • Saved providers appear on home only when the guest has saved someone.
    • More options menu (from the old hamburger): MyChart sign-in, Inbox, Shuttle, patient resource guide, patient resources, COVID-19 resources, Why UT Southwestern, physician resources, full site, social links, and privacy policy.
    • MedBlog “See all” opens a story list with topic filters. The home carousel and the list open the same stories.
    • No appointment context – that module is signed-in only.
