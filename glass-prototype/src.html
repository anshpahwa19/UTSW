UTSW MyCare home screen – glass direction

Interactive mobile prototype, 390 × 844 pt. All patients, providers, alerts, and dates are fictional sample data.

8:30

iPhone 14/15 class – 390 pt wide

Audience

Each audience has its own module composition, set by UTSW administrators.

Emergency alerts

Active alerts always render first. With two, the higher-priority alert leads and the second is one tap away. Expired alerts never show.

Accessibility and device

Both settings start from your system preferences. Reduce transparency swaps every glass surface for solid #0B3A66.

Module layout – guest home

A CMS preview: reorder or hide modules and the home updates without an app release.
