Module layout

This is the order and visibility UTSW administrators set in the CMS. Changes show on the phone right away, with no app release. Expired items hide themselves.

    Iconography

    Every icon comes from one family, Material Symbols Rounded (Google, Apache 2.0), used through a few rules. Its rounded ends match the round-capped U in the MyCare symbol. Its weight, fill, grade, and optical-size settings cover every state from one source. It also has clinical symbols, such as labs, cardiology, and medication, that general icon sets lack.

    Wells, color, and state

    • Wells are rounded squares cut to the MyCare tile’s corner (about 22%): 48 × 48 with radius 12.0 for tiles and empty states, 40 × 40 with radius 8.0 for list rows. Circles are kept for people, shown as initials.
    • Duotone only inside wells: the outline in UT Southwestern Blue over the filled shape in PMS 2995 at 60% (#66CBEC). The icon still reads without the cyan, the same way the logo’s dot turns white in one-color versions.
    • Filled means state: the selected tab, a finished action (Added), and alert severity. Play and pause are always filled, as on iOS and Android. Everything else is outline.
    • Badges sit on the icon’s top-right corner in magenta with white numerals (4.9:1). The count is also part of the button’s spoken name.
    • Color: Blue on light surfaces (8.9:1 on the tint); white on Blue, drawn at grade −25 so it doesn’t look heavier; gray #5B6770 for inactive tabs and row chevrons; magenta for the emergency triangle (4.4:1 on the alert tint); gray #333333 for the advisory circle. Cyan never appears on its own.
    • Severity by shape: emergency is a triangle, advisory is a circle, so severity reads without color.

    One meaning, one symbol

      Rules

      1. A shortcut and its destination use the same symbol. Find a doctor and the Find care tab are both the stethoscope.
      2. Every icon has a visible label, except back, close, search, notifications, refresh, and carousel controls. Those have spoken names. Icons themselves are hidden from screen readers.
      3. A trailing open_in_new arrow means the action leaves UTSW MyCare (MyChart, maps, a rideshare app). A trailing chevron means it opens a screen in the app. A down chevron expands in place.
      4. Icons are for actions and wayfinding. Campaign and story art is drawn separately and never mixed with icons.
      5. Ship the exported SVGs in design/icons/svg (one file per symbol and state). iOS: add them to the asset catalog with Preserve Vector Data turned on. Android: import them as vector drawables.

      Accessibility notes

      • Touch targets are at least 44 × 44 (buttons 48 tall, tabs 56), with at least 8.0 between them.
      • Alert severity uses a shape, a word, and color. New alerts are read out through an assertive live region.
      • Sheets and search trap focus, close with Escape, and return focus to the control that opened them.
      • Every icon button has a name; icons, art, and the status bar are hidden from screen readers.
      • The carousel is a labeled region with slide labels (“1 of 3”), previous, next, and pause controls.
