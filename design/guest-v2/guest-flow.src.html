    Requirement coverage

    Tokens and rules

    Same tokens, icon family, and logo files as the home screen prototype (design/TOKENS.md, design/icons, mycare-logo/). New in this flow:

    • Type: 12.0, 14.0, 16.0, 18.0, 20.0, 24.0, 28.0 only. Provider name 16.0/700 Blue on cards, 24.0/700 on the profile. Large text remaps up the same scale.
    • Spacing: 4.0 icon–label · 8.0 between controls · 12.0 inside cards and between results · 16.0 gutter and card padding · 24.0 between sections · 32.0 sheet footer inset · 48.0 end of scroll.
    • Filters: multi-select toggle chips show a check mark and fill when on. Within a group any match counts (OR); across groups all must match (AND). “Clear all” resets the draft; nothing applies until “Show results”. The button carries the live count.
    • Plain-language search: phrases map to specialties, conditions, languages, gender, and availability (for example “spanish speaking pediatrician”, “heart doctor”, “afib”). Misspellings within one or two letters are corrected and the correction is shown. Mapped filters appear as removable chips.
    • Location: “Use my location” asks the OS once. If permission is denied or the location can’t be found, the app says so, offers ZIP code or city entry, and keeps searching without distance.
    • States: loading uses skeleton cards (no shimmer with reduced motion); service failure and offline each name the problem and offer “Try again”; offline also points to saved providers, which work without a connection.
    • Favorites: a heart toggle (outline → filled magenta with a 300 ms pop) with a spoken name (“Save …” / “Remove … from saved”) and an Undo toast. Guest favorites live on the device only.
    • Share: opens the native share sheet with a public profile link and the provider’s name and specialty. No personal or health information is shared.
    • Profile fields: name, credentials, specialty, and at least one location are required. Bio, languages, education, research, and media show only when the directory has them; a missing section is left out rather than shown empty.
    • Motion: screen push 300 ms (slide 32 pt + fade), sheet 300 ms, chips and toggles 150–200 ms. Reduced motion turns all of it into instant changes.
    • One curve: everything that travels uses cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1), a fast start with a long, quiet settle and no overshoot. Screens 480 ms, sheets 500 ms, search 520 ms, tab highlight 460 ms, toasts 420 ms, presses 120 ms down and 320 ms back.
    • Screen transitions: a pushed screen slides in from the right edge while the previous one moves a third of the way left and dims; back reverses it with the leaving screen on top. Tab switches cross-fade with a 1.5% settle.
    • Search: the home search field expands into the full search screen and collapses back into it on close.
    • Scroll-linked motion: sections below the fold rise 64 pt, grow from 94% scale, and fade in as they travel the first third of the screen, and reverse when you scroll back. Quick-access tiles cascade by column. On first view, on-screen sections settle in with a 55 ms stagger.
    • Between sections: as a section scrolls off the top (or under a sticky header), it recedes: up to 5% smaller, 70% fainter, and 24 pt behind the scroll, so the next section appears to slide over it. Each incoming section’s heading rises out of a mask just after the section itself starts to arrive.
    • Depth: the hero copy moves at 35% of scroll speed and fades, the brand light behind it at 50%, so the hero recedes rather than scrolls; the campaign shapes drift against the card. The doctor profile’s avatar shrinks and the doctor’s name moves into the app bar once the hero leaves.
    • Surfaces: the app bar gains a shadow and the results header turns more opaque once content scrolls under them. MedBlog slides scale and dim away from the active slide.
    • Shared element: opening a doctor from a card, suggestion, or related doctor flies their avatar from the list into the profile header, growing from 56 to 72 pt and picking up the cyan ring; Back flies it home again.
    • Sheets: while a sheet is open, the screen behind steps back to 94% with rounded corners over a dark ground, as on iOS.
    • Lists that change: sorting or removing a filter glides each result card to its new place (FLIP), new cards fade in, and counts roll to their new value. Removing a saved doctor slides the card out and closes the gap.
    • Feedback: the heart fills outward from its center; the Undo toast shows a hairline countdown; pull to refresh draws a ring that turns solid when releasing will refresh; finding your location shows soft sonar rings; “Read more” opens the bio by height.
    • Pacing: the MedBlog dot fills during each 6 s dwell and advances when full; it pauses on touch, hover, or focus. The search screen’s chips and rows, Find care suggestions, and the profile header settle in with short staggers. On first open the alert banner arrives after the hero.
    • Ambient motion is limited to the slow light behind the hero (22 s). No bounces, confetti, or blinking. Reduced motion turns all of it off and shows every section in place.
